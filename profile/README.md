# Aksio

Aksio is a rational, methodical way to solve the problems in your life, with
the help of agents, in a way you can always follow.

The name comes from *axioms*. The method rests on well-established statements
about how to reason and how to solve problems.

> We are building a space for humans, where they come to solve their problems,
> and utilize the power of agents to do so. But the human should always stay in
> control.

The human is at the steering wheel. Agents do the legwork. The human decides,
and can always follow and undo what happened.

The method has three jobs:

1. **Filter** what reaches you, so only problems that qualify take your
   attention.
2. **Order** the qualified problems, so you always know what to do next.
3. **Support solving**, through a clear understanding of causes, constraints
   and preferences, reusable tools, and help from agents.
